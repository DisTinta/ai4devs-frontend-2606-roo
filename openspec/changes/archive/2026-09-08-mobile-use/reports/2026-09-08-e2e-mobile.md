## Scenario A — Desktop: horizontal stages with horizontal scroll
- Viewport 800×800 (≥ md). Measured via computed styles / bounding rects:
  `flex-direction: row`, columns share the same top (laid out horizontally), board
  `overflow-x: auto`, board `scrollWidth 800 > clientWidth 696` (horizontally scrollable), and the
  page is not broken (`document.scrollWidth ≤ innerWidth`). ✔
- Evidence: `./2026-09-08-e2e-desktop-horizontal.png`
- Note: `overflow-md-auto` (from the first draft) is not emitted by Bootstrap's default build, so it was
  changed to `overflow-x-auto`; this E2E measurement is what caught it.

## Scenario B — Mobile: stacked full-width
- Viewport 375×800 (< md). `flex-direction: column`, the three columns stack at increasing tops
  (110 / 283 / 456), each ~351px wide (≈ full 375px viewport), page not broken. ✔
- Evidence: `./2026-09-08-e2e-mobile-stacked.png`

## Scenario C — Header usable on a narrow viewport
- At 375px: the back control and the title are both visible (non-zero rects) and their bounding boxes
  do not overlap. ✔ (same screenshot as B)

## Scenario D — Touch drag moves a candidate and persists
- Emulated touch via CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`: long-press on the "Jane Smith" card (held ~280ms,
  past the 200ms activation delay), dragged onto the "Technical Interview" column, released.
- Result: the card moved to "Technical Interview"; `GET /position/1/candidates` confirmed Jane at
  `currentInterviewStepId 2` (the HU-4 stage-update `PUT` fired via touch). ✔
- Evidence: `./2026-09-08-e2e-touch-drag-moved.png`

## Scenario E — Short swipe scrolls, does not drag
- Emulated touch: touchstart on the "Carlos García" card, then moved immediately (~60ms total, well
  under the 200ms long-press delay) and released.
- Result: the card did NOT move (stayed in "Technical Interview") — the short swipe did not start a
  drag, so the activation constraint preserves normal scrolling. ✔

## Environment restore

- Final state = baseline: Jane 1, Carlos 2, John 3. Browser closed.
